How to get the file from Google
1. Open takeout.google.com → Deselect all.
2. Tick Saved (your lists, like Starred places) and/or Maps (your places) → Next step → Create export.
3. Download the .zip from Google, then pick that .zip here (no need to unzip). Maps (your places) has the exact coordinates; Saved lists often don't.
My Maps works too: export as KML (not KMZ) and pick it.
